Menu bar platform for macOS

One menu bar.
Every utility you reach for.

MenuUtils is a modular macOS menu-bar platform that consolidates the power-user tools you keep open all day — system and network monitoring, clipboard history, on-device meeting transcription, app and Xcode cleanup, and a keyboard cleaning mode — into a single, calm, native app.

The modules

What's inside

Seven modules, one host app. Each lives in the menu bar and can be turned on or off independently — actual panels from the running app, not mockups.

System Monitor

CPU, memory, disk, and GPU at a glance — with a detail window that goes as deep as Activity Monitor when you need it: per-core utilization, fan RPM, thermal state, top processes.

  • Per-core CPU + GPU utilization
  • Fan speed & temperature sensors
  • Memory purge to reclaim RAM
Direct download only

Network Monitor

Live upload/download throughput in the menu bar, with hour/day/week/month history and per-interface breakdowns whenever you switch between Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and hotspot.

  • Live speed with compaction-tiered history
  • Top apps by live download & upload
  • Per-connection-type usage totals
  • Line, bar, and area chart views

Clipboard Manager

Every copy you make, searchable and typed — links, code, colors, files, images — with a global shortcut that works from anywhere and sensitive content blurred until you ask for it.

  • Content-kind detection & filters
  • Sensitive-content blur, pinned entries
  • Global shortcut, no app switch needed

Meeting Transcription

Transcribe a call or an in-person conversation as it happens, or import a recording you already have. Your microphone and system audio are recorded as separate lanes, so what you said and what the other side said never blur together — and speech is recognized on your Mac with Apple Speech or downloadable Parakeet models.

  • Live captions, then a final pass rebuilt from the saved audio
  • Import audio & video files, re-transcribe in another language
  • Call-mode echo suppression & remote speaker separation

App Uninstaller

See every installed app sorted by size before you decide what goes. Moves the app bundle and its support files, caches, preferences, and containers to Trash. When a leftover is owned by root, an optional helper finishes the job — and when something can't be removed, you're told exactly why.

  • Size, name, or date sort across all apps
  • Support files, caches & containers included
  • Optional helper that can only move files to Trash
Direct download only

Xcode Cleaner

DerivedData, module caches, device support, and stale simulators — totalled before you delete a byte, and blocked from running while Xcode is open to keep your build state safe.

  • Reclaim total shown before cleanup
  • Blocks cleanup while Xcode is running
  • Touches only the Xcode folders it lists

Keyboard Cleaner

Wipe down your keyboard without typing into whatever is open. Clean Keyboard blocks keys, modifiers, and media keys for a timed session while the pointer stays free to hit Stop. Soft Lock covers every display until you press your unlock shortcut — a quick screen cover, not a replacement for the macOS lock screen.

  • Timed cleaning sessions, 30 seconds to 5 minutes
  • Soft Lock with a custom unlock shortcut (default ⌃⌥⌘U)
  • Fails open: sleep, screen lock, or lost access restores input
Direct download only
Menu bar

A click away

Every module opens a compact panel straight from the menu bar — live numbers and the one action you need — with Details one more click away when you want the full window.

Preferences

Yours to configure

Every module ships with its own settings — menu bar display, thresholds, and a global shortcut to open it from anywhere — plus one place to see exactly what each module can access.

Editions

Two editions, one codebase

MenuUtils ships on two lanes. The direct download is the complete product; the Mac App Store edition is sandboxed and leaves out the modules that need access the sandbox can't grant.

Privacy

Private by design

MenuUtils does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. Clipboard history, meeting recordings, and transcripts stay on your Mac, and speech is recognized on-device. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking SDKs.
