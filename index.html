MenuUtils

A menu bar platform for macOS

One menu bar. Every utility you reach for.

MenuUtils folds the power-user tools you keep open all day — system and network monitoring, clipboard history, on-device meeting transcription, app and Xcode cleanup, a keyboard cleaning mode, and a menu bar hider — into one calm, native menu-bar app.

01 The modules

Eight tools.
One status item.

Each module lives in the same menu-bar panel and switches on or off on its own. Every screenshot here is the running app — no mockups.

01/08

System Monitor

CPU, memory, disk, and GPU at a glance — with a detail window that goes as deep as Activity Monitor when you need it: per-core utilization, fan RPM, thermal state, top processes.

  • Per-core CPU + GPU utilization
  • Fan speed & temperature sensors
  • Memory purge to reclaim RAM

Direct download only

02/08

Network Monitor

Live upload and download throughput in the menu bar, with hour, day, week, and month history and per-interface breakdowns as you move between Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and hotspot.

  • Live speed with compaction-tiered history
  • Top apps by live download & upload
  • Per-connection-type usage totals
  • Line, bar, and area chart views

03/08

Clipboard Manager

Every copy you make, searchable and typed — links, code, colors, files, images — behind a global shortcut that works from anywhere, with sensitive content blurred until you ask for it.

  • Content-kind detection & filters
  • Sensitive-content blur, pinned entries
  • Global shortcut, no app switch needed

04/08

Meeting Transcription

Transcribe a call or an in-person conversation as it happens, or import a recording you already have. Your microphone and system audio are kept as separate lanes, so what you said and what the other side said never blur together — and speech is recognized on your Mac with Apple Speech or downloadable Parakeet models.

  • Live captions, then a final pass rebuilt from the saved audio
  • Import audio & video files, re-transcribe in another language
  • Call-mode echo suppression & remote speaker separation

05/08

App Uninstaller

See every installed app sorted by size before you decide what goes. It moves the app bundle and its support files, caches, preferences, and containers to Trash. When a leftover is owned by root, an optional helper finishes the job — and when something can't be removed, you're told exactly why.

  • Size, name, or date sort across all apps
  • Support files, caches & containers included
  • Optional helper that can only move files to Trash

Direct download only

06/08

Xcode Cleaner

DerivedData, module caches, device support, and stale simulators — totalled before you delete a byte, and blocked while Xcode is open to keep your build state safe.

  • Reclaim total shown before cleanup
  • Blocks cleanup while Xcode is running
  • Touches only the Xcode folders it lists

07/08

Keyboard Cleaner

Wipe down your keyboard without typing into whatever is open. Clean Keyboard blocks keys, modifiers, and media keys for a timed session while the pointer stays free to hit Stop. Soft Lock covers every display until you press your unlock shortcut — a quick screen cover, not a replacement for the macOS lock screen.

  • Timed cleaning sessions, 30 seconds to 5 minutes
  • Soft Lock with a custom unlock shortcut (default ⌃⌥⌘U)
  • Fails open: sleep, screen lock, or lost access restores input

Direct download only

08/08 Next release

Menu Bar Hider

Too many icons up top? MenuUtils adds an arrow to the menu bar. ⌘-drag the icons you rarely need to its left, and one click hides them — another brings them back. Add a divider for icons that should stay hidden until you Option-click the arrow.

  • Hide on launch, or hide again after a delay
  • Optional always-hidden section behind a divider
  • A global shortcut to hide or show from anywhere
  • Chevron, eye, dot, or sidebar arrow styles

    05 Private by design

    Your clipboard, your meetings, your keystrokes — none of it leaves your Mac.

    • No accountsNothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
    • No analyticsNo usage telemetry and no uploaded crash reports.
    • No tracking SDKsNo advertising, no third-party trackers of any kind.
    • On-device speechAudio and transcripts are recognized and stored locally.

    The only times MenuUtils goes online

    • Update checksDirect download only. A standard HTTPS request to this site's update feed.
    • Speech models you chooseDownloaded once from Hugging Face, then run entirely on your Mac.
