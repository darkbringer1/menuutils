Menu bar platform for macOS

One menu bar.
Every utility you reach for.

MenuUtils is a modular macOS menu-bar platform that consolidates the power-user tools you keep open all day — system monitoring, meeting transcription, clipboard history, app uninstaller, and Xcode cleanup — into a single, calm, native app.

The modules

What's inside

Six modules, one host app. Each lives in the menu bar and can be turned on or off independently — actual panels from the running app, not mockups.

System Monitor

CPU, memory, disk, and GPU at a glance — with a detail window that goes as deep as Activity Monitor when you need it: per-core utilization, fan RPM, thermal state, top processes.

  • Per-core CPU + GPU utilization
  • Fan speed & temperature sensors
  • Memory purge to reclaim RAM

Network Monitor

Live upload/download throughput in the menu bar, with hour/day/week/month history and per-interface breakdowns whenever you switch between Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and hotspot.

  • Live speed with compaction-tiered history
  • Per-connection-type usage totals
  • Line, bar, and area chart views

Clipboard Manager

Every copy you make, searchable and typed — links, code, colors, files, images — with a global shortcut that works from anywhere and sensitive content blurred until you ask for it.

  • Content-kind detection & filters
  • Sensitive-content blur, pinned entries
  • Global shortcut, no app switch needed

Meeting Transcription

Capture your microphone and system audio as separate sources, follow the transcript live, and keep a durable local history for calls and focused work sessions.

  • Independent microphone and system-audio capture
  • Local speech-to-text with source labels
  • Searchable, timestamped transcript history

App Uninstaller

See every installed app sorted by size before you decide what goes. Moves the app bundle and its support files, caches, preferences, and containers to Trash — user-selected folders only.

  • Size, name, or date sort across all apps
  • Support files, caches & containers included
  • Nothing scanned outside folders you grant

Xcode Cleaner

DerivedData, module caches, device support, and stale simulators — totalled before you delete a byte, and blocked from running while Xcode is open to keep your build state safe.

  • Reclaim total shown before cleanup
  • Blocks cleanup while Xcode is running
  • Reaches caches only via a granted bookmark
Preferences

Yours to configure

Every module ships with its own settings — menu bar display, thresholds, shortcuts — plus one place to see exactly what each module can access.
