Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06

MenuUtils is a macOS menu-bar utility. The short version: MenuUtils does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your Mac. The app only goes online to check for updates and to download speech models you choose to use.

Data we do not collect

MenuUtils does not collect, aggregate, sell, share, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, account details)
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or product telemetry
  • Crash reports (none are uploaded by the app)
  • Location data
  • Contact or calendar data
  • Any information about you to any third party

The app has no accounts, no login, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data stored locally on your Mac

Some features store data on your Mac so they can function. This data never leaves your device:

The Clipboard Manager and Meeting Transcription features can hold sensitive content (for example, text you copy, or meeting audio you transcribe). This content is processed and stored only on your Mac. You can delete it at any time from within each module — deleting a meeting removes both its transcript and its audio recordings.

On-device processing

All processing happens on your Mac:

  • Speech recognition in Meeting Transcription runs locally. Your microphone and your Mac's system audio are captured as separate tracks, then transcribed with Apple's Speech framework (with on-device recognition required) or with Parakeet models running on your Mac through Core ML. Speaker separation also runs locally. Audio and transcripts are never sent to a server.
  • Imported recordings are copied into the meeting's local archive (audio only — video frames are not kept) and transcribed the same way.
  • File inspection (App Uninstaller, Xcode Cleaner) only looks at application bundles, their support files, and Xcode developer folders. In the Mac App Store edition, it can only read folders you explicitly grant through macOS security-scoped bookmarks. In the direct download, deeper scanning uses the optional Full Disk Access and App Management permissions described below.
  • Network Monitor reads per-app byte counters from the macOS nettop tool to show which apps use the network. It never inspects the content of your traffic.
  • Keyboard Cleaner (direct download) sees key events only to block them and to recognize your unlock shortcut. Keystrokes are not recorded or stored.
  • Menu Bar Hider (direct download, macOS 27) reads where menu bar icons sit so the right ones are hidden. Nothing about those icons or their apps is stored or sent anywhere.

Each permission is requested only when you enable the relevant feature, and each can be revoked at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Required Reason API declarations

MenuUtils accesses a small number of macOS APIs that Apple requires to be declared in a privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy). The approved reasons are:

  • UserDefaults (CA92.1) — storing the app's own preferences.
  • Disk space (85F4.1) — displaying free and used disk space to you in System Monitor.
  • File timestamps (DDA9.1, 3B52.1) — showing and sorting by "last modified" in App Uninstaller, on files inside folders you granted access to.

None of the information accessed through these APIs is sent off-device.

Network connections

MenuUtils makes only the connections below. None of them send personal data, clipboard content, audio, or transcripts.

Speech model downloads

Apple Speech uses language assets that macOS downloads and manages from Apple. If you choose a Parakeet model, or turn on speaker identification, MenuUtils downloads the model files from Hugging Face when you first use them. These are standard HTTPS file downloads; like any web request, they reveal your IP address to the host. After a model is downloaded, it runs entirely on your Mac.

Software updates (direct-distribution build only)

The version of MenuUtils distributed directly from our website checks for updates using Sparkle. To check for updates, the app makes a standard HTTPS request to the update feed URL on our website. The only information exposed by this request is standard HTTP metadata (such as your IP address and a generic user-agent string) that any web request reveals to the host. No usage data, identifiers, or clipboard/transcript content is ever sent.

The Mac App Store version does not include this update mechanism; it updates through the App Store.

Children's privacy

MenuUtils is a developer/utility tool and is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes, the app will link to the updated policy from within the app.
